Brisk Dex Player Guide

Everything you need to use Brisk Dex with Pokémon Brisk Emerald. Open a section below whenever you need help — no developer knowledge required.

Getting Started
  1. Open Brisk Dex and choose Load .sav / .srm file.
  2. Select your Pokémon Brisk Emerald save file.
  3. Brisk Dex reads the save locally and fills in your trainer, party, boxes, and Pokédex information.
  4. Use the tabs across the top to browse encounters, Pokémon, trainers, moves, abilities, items, and other features.
Brisk Dex uses Brisk Emerald data rather than assuming the original Emerald or standard National Pokédex data.
Loading & Saving

Changes save automatically. Desktop and Android update the opened file. Browser and iOS sessions keep a recovery copy inside Brisk Dex; export a copy when you need to use it in an emulator.

  • .sav and .srm are supported save-file extensions.
  • The app checks the save structure before displaying editable data.
  • Your last-used save reopens automatically. If an automatic save fails, the app displays the error.
Keep a backup of an important save before making large edits.
Pokémon & PC Boxes

The first tab displays your party and Brisk Emerald PC boxes. Select a Pokémon to view its information and available editing options.

  • Pokémon can be dragged between supported storage tiles.
  • Brisk Emerald saves can use the expanded 20-box layout.
  • Shiny Pokémon are visually marked.
  • Species, moves, forms, abilities, and types are interpreted using Brisk Emerald's data.
External Storage

External Storage is Brisk Dex storage that exists outside a single game save. It is useful for keeping Pokémon available between saves.

  • Drag a party or boxed Pokémon into External Storage to store it.
  • Drag a stored Pokémon onto a compatible empty box slot to withdraw it.
  • Use search and sorting to find stored Pokémon quickly.
  • Export an archive if you want a separate copy of your Brisk Dex storage.
Brisk Pokédex

The Pokédex combines Brisk Emerald species information with progress read from your save.

  • None: no Pokédex progress is currently recorded.
  • Seen: the Pokémon has been seen.
  • Caught: the Pokémon has been obtained.
  • Shiny: a shiny copy is present or the status has been marked manually.
  • Entries include Brisk stats, abilities, evolutions/forms, movepools, locations, sprites, and cries when available.

You can manually change a displayed Pokédex status in Brisk Dex after the save has been read.

Current Brisk Emerald Progression

Brisk Emerald's current gym progression level caps are:

25 → 35 → 45 → 50 → 55 → 65 → 75 → 100

  • The current level cap is also shown in Brisk Emerald's pause menu.
  • Overworld Poké Ball catches are restricted to Pokémon at or below your current progression cap.
  • If every wild Pokémon on the current route is above your cap, the game reports that the route is too high level.
  • The battle Bag shows catch chance beside the Poké Ball pocket and rounds the displayed percentage to the nearest whole number.
These values reflect the current Brisk Emerald build and can be updated again as progression is rebalanced.
Route Encounters & Locations

Route Encounters shows where Pokémon can be found in the current Brisk Emerald data.

  • Filter by walking, surfing, fishing rods, Rock Smash, static encounters, or gifts.
  • Search by location, Pokémon, or type.
  • Related floors and subareas may be grouped under a cleaner player-facing location name.
  • Encounter information can differ greatly from normal Pokémon Emerald.
Trainer Teams

Trainer Teams lets you look up opponents before or after battling them in Brisk Emerald.

  • Search by trainer, class, Pokémon, move, ability, item, or location.
  • Each Pokémon shows the information specific to that trainer's actual team member.
  • Bosses and special trainers can differ substantially from vanilla Emerald.
Moves, Abilities & Items

These tabs are reference dexes built from Brisk Emerald itself.

  • Moves: descriptions, type, category, battle data, and Pokémon that can learn them.
  • Abilities: descriptions and Pokémon that can have each ability.
  • Items: descriptions, locations, and the #ID used by Brisk Emerald's debug item selection.
Bag

The Bag shows items from your loaded save. External Item Storage holds deposited stacks. Key items stay in the game.

  • Search stored items by name or displayed #ID.
  • Use the pocket subtabs to browse Poké Balls, medicine, berries, TMs/HMs, battle items, held items, key items, and other items.
  • Drag a stack or select Move, then choose the quantity. Key items cannot be transferred. Brisk Dex does not create items or increase quantities.
Competitive Sets

Competitive Sets provides suggested builds using each Pokémon's current Brisk stats, typing, abilities, and legal moves.

These are starting points rather than a guarantee that a set is the single best option for every battle. Brisk balance changes can make standard-game sets outdated.

Online Battles

Online Battle lets two Brisk Dex players host or join a private room.

  1. Choose up to six Pokémon from your party, PC boxes, or External Storage.
  2. One player selects Host Battle and shares the six-character room code.
  3. The other player enters the same relay and room code, then selects Join Battle.
  4. Both players ready up and play the battle from Brisk Dex.

The available battle mechanics are those currently implemented by the Brisk Dex simulator, which may not yet reproduce every Brisk Emerald mechanic exactly.

Wonder Trade

Wonder Trade exchanges an eligible Pokémon for another eligible Pokémon. Special species that are excluded by Brisk's Wonder Trade rules cannot be submitted.

Treat a Wonder Trade as a real trade: do not send a Pokémon you intend to keep unless you already have another copy or backup.
FAQ & Troubleshooting

Why does Brisk Dex disagree with a normal Pokédex website?
Brisk Emerald changes Pokémon, trainers, encounters, moves, abilities, items, and other mechanics, so Brisk Dex prioritizes Brisk Emerald's own extracted data.

Why is my save not loading?
Make sure you selected the actual emulator/game save file rather than a save state, ROM, ZIP, or unrelated file.

Why did an edit not appear in game?
Make sure you saved the changes from Brisk Dex and then loaded the resulting save in your emulator or game.

Why is a sprite, cry, description, or location missing?
The current Brisk data bundle may not contain that asset or field yet. Updating Brisk Dex and its Brisk Emerald reference data may resolve it.

Does External Storage belong to my Emerald save?
No. It is Brisk Dex storage kept separately from an individual game save.

Can I use boxed or externally stored Pokémon online?
Yes. The Online Battle team picker can use eligible Pokémon from the party, PC boxes, or External Storage.
